What we build

Four kinds of work

Scope is written down before work starts. Pricing is agreed per project.

  1. 01

    AI assistants and agents

    Chat and voice assistants for a site, a phone line or an internal tool, with a scoped prompt, a server-side filter and rate limits.

  2. 02

    Multi-agent systems and protocols

    Several models or agents working one problem: hand-off formats that carry context between them, review loops, and a record of who decided what.

  3. 03

    AI workflow review

    How your team’s AI agents and workflows are controlled today: who approves what, what gets logged, what runs without a human. Written findings and a ranked fix list.

  4. 04

    Platforms and integration

    Web apps, APIs and data pipelines that put a model inside the systems you already run, from retrieval over your own documents to deployment.
