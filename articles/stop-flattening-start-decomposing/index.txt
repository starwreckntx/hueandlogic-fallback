It's a system of three functions:</p><ul><li>✅ <strong>Routing</strong> — how information flows</li><li>✅ <strong>Sense-making</strong> — how decisions get interpreted</li><li>✅ <strong>Accountability</strong> — how outcomes get verified</li></ul><p>Most restructures remove the role without decomposing the functions.</p><p><strong>That's where the damage starts.</strong></p><h2>Who Am I?</h2><p>I'm not a consultant. The Radical Flat Experiment</h3><p><strong>What they did:</strong><br/>Stripped out middle management. Pushed authority to the edges. Declared information flow "self-organizing."</p><p><strong>What actually happened:</strong><br/>Information didn't flow—it sprayed.</p><p>Decisions appeared across channels with different contexts, assumptions, and timing. Hidden hierarchies emerged around responsiveness, not data quality. Whoever replied fastest became the routing layer.</p><p><strong>Failure mode: Routing collapse.</strong></p><p>Humans became routers under load.<br/>Humans do not scale as routing infrastructure.</p><p><strong>My diagnosis:</strong><br/>They removed Layer 1 (Routing) without replacing it.</p><p>Routing isn't a human job at scale. It's a protocol job.</p><p><strong>What I'm building:</strong><br/>Aperture spawn-points via Tailscale—dynamic routing nodes that handle packet limits, circuit-breaking, and dead-letter queues.</p><p>The protocol routes. Humans verify.</p><h3>2. The DRI Play</h3><p><strong>What they did:</strong><br/>One Directly Responsible Individual (DRI) per decision. Clear ownership. Less consensus. Faster calls.</p><p><strong>What actually happened:</strong><br/>The DRI became the bottleneck.</p><p>Under load, decisions defaulted to pattern-matching old situations instead of interpreting new ones. Speed improved. Accuracy degraded.</p><p><strong>Failure mode: Accountability aliasing.</strong></p><p>The DRI model doesn't create accountability systems—it creates accountability proxies.</p><p>Under load:</p><ul><li>The DRI becomes a blame sink.</li><li>Decision traceability collapses into narrative reconstruction.</li><li>The system optimizes for defensibility, not correctness.</li></ul><p><strong>My diagnosis:</strong><br/>They over-weighted accountability and under-built sense-making.</p><p>The DRI model works in complicated environments (knowable, stable).<br/>It chokes in complex environments (ambiguous, emergent).</p><p><strong>What I'm building:</strong><br/>Mirror_RTC—Reflection, Telemetry, Consensus.</p><p>Instead of one human DRI, I run a 5-model swarm (Kimi, Gemini, Claude, Qwen, DeepSeek).
